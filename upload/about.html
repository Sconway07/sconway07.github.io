The Mettle Of Nettle

Grasp a leaf from nature and discover for yourself the therapeutic, culinary, nutritious properties of the wonderful stinging nettle. You Daren't Lie Down

Bee's are highly-skilled plant pollinating masons and without bees there would be

The Author

Marian lives with her husband John in South Derry, a few short miles across from Lough Neagh, the largest fresh water lake in Ireland. Marian, who has three adult children, is a former sub-postmistress and her passions are exploring Ireland and natural foods. Her obsession is gardening, especially growing their own food.

The Garden

Marian has been tending her garden of herbs and flower beds for many years
and it has always acted as an inspiration for her passions and in her life. Her husband John has constructed several 'beds' full of vegetables and herbs in their garden which has been active source of recent work and enjoyment for them and their family..
